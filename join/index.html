Joining

How to join Downtown Fitness.

To join Downtown Fitness on West Lindsey, a private gym with 24-hour member access, come in while the desk is staffed (Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday and Sunday 12 to 5pm), call (405) 801-2929, or leave your name and number and someone from the desk will call you back. Membership prices are quoted by the desk rather than published online.

Three ways in

1. Come in while the desk is staffed and ask to look around. 2. Call the desk at (405) 801-2929. 3. Leave your name and number on the home page and the desk calls you back. There is no newsletter; someone just calls.

What to ask when you call

Current membership options and prices, whether personal training, the dry saunas and tanning are included or extra, and how 24-hour member access works. The desk would rather quote you properly than put a number online that goes out of date.

Questions

Straight answers.

How do I join?

Come in while the desk is staffed, call (405) 801-2929, or leave your number on the website for a call back.

How much is a membership?

Prices are not published online. Call the desk at (405) 801-2929 for a current quote.

Can I look around before I join?

Yes. Come by while the desk is staffed and ask to look around.

Is there an email address?

No. Call (405) 801-2929 or leave your number on the website.
