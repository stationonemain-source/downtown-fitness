Locally owned and operated

A locally owned gym in Norman, not a franchise.

Downtown Fitness on West Lindsey is a private gym in Norman, OK, locally owned and operated by owner Mark Floyd. It is not part of a franchise or chain. It is at 1610 W Lindsey St and is rated 4.7 stars from 83 Google reviews.

What local ownership changes

Decisions get made on the floor, not at a head office. The owner is someone you will actually meet. Reviewers mention it by name: "Mark (the owner) and his staff keep the place spotless," and "the staff and owner make you feel right at home."

The owner's own story

In December 2017, Mark weighed around 310 pounds. He has written about getting down to around 200 and a size 32 waist, and about the Downtown Fitness family that encouraged him. His full story, in his words, is on the home page.
