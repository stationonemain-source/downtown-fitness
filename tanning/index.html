Train and tan in one stop

The tanning booths are in the same building as the gym floor, the locker rooms and the dry saunas, and tanning is included with your membership, like the saunas, so there is no second stop and no second bill.

Stand-up, not a bed

These are stand-up booths: you stand inside a ring of lamps instead of lying on a bed.

Before your first session

Ask at the desk about session length and what is right for your skin type, and always wear eye protection. Tanning is not recommended for everyone; if you are unsure, check with your doctor first.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does Downtown Fitness have tanning?

Yes, stand-up high-pressure tanning booths, inside the gym at 1610 W Lindsey St, Norman, OK.

How much is tanning?

Tanning is included with membership. There is no separate charge for members.

Are they tanning beds or booths?

Stand-up booths. You stand inside the booth rather than lying on a bed.

Do I need to be a gym member to tan?

Yes. Tanning is for members, and it is included with membership.
