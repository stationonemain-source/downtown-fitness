What "private" means for you

A private gym is a members' gym, not a walk-in franchise floor, and members can train 24 hours a day. In practice that means a cleaner floor, less waiting for equipment, and a staff who get to know the people who train there.

Reviewers say the same thing in their own words. One calls the cleanliness "second to none"; another says the gym "has a family feel to it where you get to know people."

What is in the building

A cardio hall with treadmills, bikes and ellipticals under the TVs. A full selectorized machine floor. A free-weight room with racks, lifting platforms, bumper plates and dumbbells to 100 lb. Locker rooms. Dry saunas. Stand-up high-pressure tanning booths. And NASM-certified personal training on the same floor.
