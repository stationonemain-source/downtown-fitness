From the owner

310 pounds. Then 200.

In December 2017, I weighed around 310 pounds and am the face of Downtown Fitness on West Lindsey. I was honestly uncomfortable within my own skin and it hurt physically and mentally to workout.

This particular pair of jeans are size 44 waist, but I could never fit into them because they were too small. I have been on a mission to get my health as well as my life back. I’m currently at around 200 pounds, wearing a size 32 waist and I’m back in the gym, feeling the energy that I’ve missed for far too long.

I have had so much encouragement from friends, family and the Downtown Fitness family and I thank all of you. Most importantly I thank God for guiding my path.

For those of you out there that are where I was: you can do it.

Come join me and the Downtown Fitness family.

Mark Floyd · Owner, Downtown Fitness

Before you come in

Straight answers.

When is the desk staffed?

Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, and Saturday and Sunday 12 to 5pm. Come in during those hours to sign up, ask about personal training or tanning, or just look around.

What does a membership cost?

Call the desk on (405) 801-2929 or come in and ask. We would rather quote you properly than put a number here that is out of date.

Can I look around first?

Yes. It is a private gym, so come by during staffed hours and someone at the desk will show you the floor. No appointment needed.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes: nationally trained personal training, one-on-one. Ask at the desk or call and we will set it up.

What is actually in the building?

A cardio hall, a full selectorized machine floor, a free-weight room with racks, platforms and dumbbells to 100 lb, locker rooms, dry saunas and high-pressure tanning booths.

Where do I park?

There is a lot straight out front on West Lindsey, a few minutes from campus.

Why West Lindsey

Locally owned and operated.

A private gym a few minutes from campus, run by the people who own it. Not a franchise and not a crowd: a clean floor, trainers who know you, and an owner you will actually meet.
