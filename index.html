Before you come in

Straight answers.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Every hour of every day, all year. The front desk is staffed during the day; the rest of the time the door is open and the floor is yours.

What does a membership cost?

Call the desk on (405) 801-2929 or come in and ask. We would rather quote you properly than put a number here that is out of date.

Can I look around first?

Come in any time and walk the floor. The door is open around the clock, so you do not need an appointment to see the place.

Do you have trainers?

Yes, and they hold national certification. Ask at the desk or call and we will set it up.

What is actually in the building?

One floor: a cardio hall, a full selectorized machine floor, a free-weight room with racks, platforms and dumbbells to 100 lb, locker rooms, and a cedar sauna.

Where do I park?

There is a lot straight out front on West Lindsey, a few minutes from campus.

Why West Lindsey

A neighborhood gym, not a franchise.

One floor a few minutes from campus. Open every hour of every day, because the people who train here do not keep the same hours. The door is never locked.
